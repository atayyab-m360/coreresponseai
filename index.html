Why CORE AI

Why start with a conversation instead of a proposal?

Before starting CORE AI, I spent roughly 17 years in organ and tissue donation — including years performing surgical recovery procedures myself before moving into regional operational leadership. It's one thing to manage a process from behind a desk; it's another to have actually done the work you're now responsible for coordinating. That environment left no room for guessing — timing, communication, documentation, and process all had to work together, and changing one piece without understanding what it affected upstream and downstream could create an entirely new problem.

That's the lesson I carried into CORE AI: understand the whole system before you touch any one piece of it — which is why we start with discovery, not a predetermined solution.
